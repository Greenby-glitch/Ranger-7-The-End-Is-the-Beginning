# Ranger-7-The-End-Is-the-Beginning

This is the public version of EBA-07's interactive educational storybook. It keeps the 31 scenes, the interactive library guide, the ten-question EBA test, historical research archive, page-turning effects, lunar approach and NASA/JPL image gallery.

Opening the website
The site can be deployed as static files to GitHub Pages. Upload index.html, sources.html, css/, js/ and assets/ to the repository root. For local preview, open index.html in a browser or use a local web server.

No separate JavaScript installation or network connection is needed for the included historical images. The source credits link to original NASA/JPL archives.

Credits and use of AI
Human work: Emiliano, Bryan and Alejandra (EBA-07) researched and structured the historical and scientific content, developed the scenes and interactive narrative, and contributed to the code. Emiliano provided an existing initial programming base. Team members reviewed, edited and integrated code and creative decisions.

AI-assisted work: AI was used in selected code-writing, organization and revision tasks. The EBA-07 emblem and EBA robot character are AI-generated artworks, directed and selected by the team. Digital schematic illustrations are educational reconstructions and not NASA originals. An AI contribution does not mean the entire program or its scientific research was automatically generated.

Sources: NASA Science, NASA History, Jet Propulsion Laboratory, NASA's Space Science Data Coordinated Archive, Lunar and Planetary Institute and Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum are cited through sources.html and the image gallery. Photographs and historical source material are distinct from the project's illustrations. EBA's conversational responses are scripted; the optional reading voice uses the browser's speech service.

Internal research drafts and unpublished production materials are not part of this public repository.

Code organization
index.html: book, library, scene layout and navigation
sources.html: verified source bibliography, media credits and transparent AI disclosure
css/style.css: design system and page transition styling
js/scenes.js: 31 scenes and source IDs
js/app.js: interaction, navigation and page turns
js/archive.js: technical and historical notes
js/resources.js: citations and image credits
js/visuals.js: illustrative scene assets and artwork placement
js/guide.js: EBA's scripted conversation
js/approach.js and js/moon.js: final approach and 3D Moon
assets/: logo, mascot and historical/local image assets
The project is independent and not endorsed by NASA.

Final mission test
The optional ten-question knowledge test is available after scene 8.4. The quiz wording was drafted with AI assistance using the team's documentary research and reviewed for publication. Seven correct answers earn the Lunar Explorer badge. EBA reacts with animated AI-generated artwork and browser-based speech. Quiz voice can be disabled. Selected technical schematics are identified as AI-assisted; historical NASA imagery is credited separately.

Publication
All paths are relative. No build step, installer, Windows launcher or private research documents are required. The optional narrator is user-activated. EBA speaks from her guide appearances only when the visitor enables voice, and the quiz has a separate voice toggle.
